Three things happen every time someone tips food into the bin.

Built for the pace of a real kitchen: the cook does nothing new except press one of three big buttons.

1

Weigh

A load cell under the bin insert records the weight of each tip on its own, not just the total at the end of the day.

2

Recognise

The camera compares before and after, cuts out only what was added and names it. Everything runs on site, no images leave the kitchen.

3

Act

The dashboard shows where the money goes, split into preparation, spoiled stock and plate leftovers, and suggests one change for tomorrow.
